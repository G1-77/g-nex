User flow — home to first trade
1
Lands on Home
Balance banner + story cards + ticker strip. Familiar — feels exactly like Binance home. Trust established immediately.
2
Taps Markets tab
Nav morphs from 4 icons into the floating trade bar anchored to BTC. All 5 assets visible in a clean table. No overwhelm.
3
Taps BTC row
Opens Asset Detail: full TradingView chart, sentiment bar, community posts. Trade bar switches to full Buy/Sell buttons for BTC.
4
Taps Buy — converts
KES amount input, M-Pesa reference, confirm. One smooth flow. No page jumps. Portfolio updates in Wallet instantly.
Design recommendations
Nav morphing — do it with CSS, not unmounting
Animate the bar height and content, never destroy and remount it
Use a single persistent bottom bar component with a CSS class swap driven by the current route. The standard 4-icon nav slides down (translateY) as the trade bar slides up — 250ms ease-out. This keeps the bar in the DOM so transition feels continuous, not jarring. On Markets route: class="trade-mode". On all others: class="nav-mode". One component, two visual states.
CSS class swap Single DOM node No unmount/remount
Home page — copy Binance's information hierarchy exactly
Balance → ticker → stories → asset table. This order is proven.
The Binance home works because it answers three questions in order: how much do I have, what is moving right now, where can I act. Replicate this hierarchy: (1) KES balance card with Deposit CTA, (2) horizontal ticker strip of 5 assets, (3) story cards for the day's 3 top movers, (4) full asset table below. The social layer (feed, posts, squad activity) lives on a dedicated tab — not on the home page. Keep home purely financial.
Balance first Ticker second Social on separate tab
Explore — TradingView's pattern, adapted for 5 assets
Discovery, not just a second market list
TradingView's Explore is not a duplicate of the market table — it is discovery-first. For GNex: top section = category pills (Trending, Gainers, Losers, Squads, Intel). Below that = rich asset cards with 7-day sparklines and community sentiment bar built in. Below that = Top Traders leaderboard strip. Below that = Active Squad Pools. The key distinction: Explore shows context and story around each asset. Markets shows raw price data. They serve different user intents — one is browsing, one is acting.
Sparklines in cards Sentiment bar visible Not a duplicate markets list
The floating trade bar — which asset does it show?
Context-aware: follows the last asset the user touched
When the user first lands on Markets, the trade bar defaults to BTC (highest trust asset, most familiar). When the user taps an asset row, the trade bar updates to that asset — BTC becomes ETH, or SOL. This means the buy/sell action is always one tap away from whatever the user just showed interest in. Store the "focused asset" in a Zustand atom. The trade bar subscribes to it. This is the single most important interaction pattern on the platform.
Default: BTC Updates on row tap Zustand atom for focus state
Thumb zone — every primary action within reach
The bottom 40% of the screen is where thumbs live
Design every conversion action (Buy, Deposit, Set Alert, Follow Trader) to live below the visual midpoint of the screen. The floating trade bar already achieves this. For asset detail: the chart takes the top 50% of the screen, sentiment + community takes the middle 25%, and the Buy/Sell sticky bar lives at the very bottom. This matches how users naturally hold a phone — they scroll up to read, they tap down to act.
Actions at bottom 40% Min tap target 44px No CTAs above fold midpoint
